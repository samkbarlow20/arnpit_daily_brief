Privacy Policy — Flipper (Google Connection)

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Overview

Flipper is a small personal assistant that reads your Google Calendar and Gmail to prepare your daily brief.

What we collect

  • Your Google Calendar events (schedule, times, locations, descriptions).
  • Your Gmail messages (subjects, senders, bodies, thread context).
  • Access is limited to the calendar and gmail.readonly scopes.

How we use it

  • To prepare your daily Morning Brief (school, work, real estate, opportunities, reminders).
  • Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than preparing your brief.

Where the data lives

Calendar and email are stored by Google. Flipper only reads them during your daily brief run and does not keep message bodies long-term.

Who operates it

Operated by you, locally, through a personal Discord bot running on your machine.

Your rights

You can revoke the connection at any time in Google's account settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Reply to your private Discord with any question. We do not sell or share your personal information.
