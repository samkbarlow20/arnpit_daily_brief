Flipper

A small personal assistant running on your machine.

What it is

Flipper reads your Google Calendar and Gmail to prepare your daily brief (school, work, real estate, opportunities, and reminders). It runs as a Discord-hosted assistant on your machine and only acts when you ask it to.

What it needs

Your Google account, for reading your Calendar and Gmail. Nothing is changed, deleted, or shared.

What it never does

  • Sends email
  • Posts to social media
  • Sells or shares your data
Why there's a homepage and privacy policy: Google requires a real, reachable site plus a privacy policy before an app can be published. These files are what you copy onto GitHub Pages to satisfy that requirement.
